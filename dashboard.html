Analysis Explanation

Technical detail

Standard Model: The Leafy Powder Quality Index (LPQI) utilizes a normalized difference approach primarily contrasting the red edge (680–730nm) against near-infrared reflectance (760–860nm).

High reflectance in the NIR coupled with strong absorption at 680nm indicates intact cellular structures and high chlorophyll concentration, typical of fresh, high-quality powder. Degradation from heat or aging flattens this curve, lowering the index.
